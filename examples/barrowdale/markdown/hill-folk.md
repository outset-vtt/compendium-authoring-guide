# Hill Folk

- Type: Species
- Id: hill-folk
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Attribution: Original Outset VTT example content, MIT license. References the separately attributed SRD Fire Bolt and Light entries.
- Size: Medium
- Speed: 30 feet
- Darkvision: 60 feet

Hill folk herd sheep and dig for iron in the uplands above Barrowdale. Long winters in sod houses have made them hardy, and every family keeps a hearth gift passed from grandparent to grandchild.

## Trait: Hardy

- Hit point maximum: +1 per level

## Choice: Hearth Gift

- Choose: 1
- Spellcasting ability: Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma

### Option: Hearthfire

- Spell: Fire Bolt (level 1)

### Option: Hearthlight

- Spell: Light (level 1)
