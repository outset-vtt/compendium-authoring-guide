# Goblin Raider

- Type: Monster
- Id: goblin-raider
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Attribution: Original Outset VTT example content, MIT license.
- Size: Small
- Creature type: Fey
- Alignment: Chaotic Neutral
- Armor Class: 15
- Hit Points: 10 (3d6)
- Speed: 30 feet
- Skills: Stealth +6
- Senses: Darkvision 60 feet, Passive Perception 9
- Languages: Common, Goblin
- Challenge: 1/4 (50 XP)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 14 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 8 |

Goblin raiders slip down from the barrow hills at dusk to steal sheep, tools and anything left unguarded in Barrowdale.

## Trait: Nimble Escape

The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action on each of its turns.

## Action: Scimitar

- Attack: Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target
- Hit: 1d6 + 2 slashing damage

## Action: Shortbow

- Attack: Ranged, +4 to hit, range 80/320 feet, one target
- Hit: 1d6 + 2 piercing damage
