# Keen Watcher

- Type: Feat
- Id: keen-watcher
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Category: General
- Prerequisite: Level 4+
- Ability score increase: +1 Wisdom or Constitution

You have stood the night watch on the dale's walls, and little escapes your notice.

## Passive: Keen Eye

- Effect: +2 to ranged weapon attack rolls
- Effect: Advantage on Perception checks

## Bonus action: Steady Nerves

- Target: Self
- Effect: Gain 5 temporary hit points

## Trait: Night Watch

You need only four hours of sleep to gain the benefit of a Long Rest.
