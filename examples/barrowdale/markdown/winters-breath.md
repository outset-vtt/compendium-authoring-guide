# Winter's Breath

- Type: Spell
- Id: winters-breath
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Level: 2
- School: Evocation
- Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
- Casting time: Action
- Range: Self
- Components: V, S, M (a pinch of frost gathered before dawn)
- Save: Constitution, half damage on a success
- Area: 15-foot cone
- Damage: 3d8 cold
- Higher levels: +1d8 damage per slot level above 2

A blast of grave-cold air roars from your outstretched hands. Water in the area freezes over, and small flames go out.
