# Barrow Wight

- Type: Monster
- Id: barrow-wight
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Attribution: Original Outset VTT example content, MIT license.
- Size: Medium
- Creature type: Undead
- Alignment: Neutral Evil
- Armor Class: 16 (chain mail)
- Hit Points: 90 (12d8 + 36)
- Speed: 30 feet
- Saving throws: Constitution +6, Wisdom +4
- Skills: Perception +4
- Senses: Darkvision 60 feet
- Languages: The languages it knew in life
- Challenge: 5 (1,800 XP)
- Legendary actions: 2
- Legendary resistance: 2/Day

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 14 | 16 | 10 | 12 | 15 |

The chieftain buried beneath the great barrow still wears his rusted mail and guards his grave goods against the living.

## Trait: Sunlight Sensitivity

While in sunlight, the wight has Disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks.

## Action: Multiattack

The wight makes one Barrow Blade attack and one Grave Grasp attack.

## Action: Barrow Blade

- Attack: Melee, +7 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target
- Hit: 1d8 + 4 slashing damage

## Action: Grave Grasp

- Attack: Melee, +7 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target
- Hit: 2d6 + 4 necrotic damage
- On hit: Grappled (escape DC 14)

## Action: Grave Chill

- Uses: Recharge 5-6
- Save: Constitution 14, half damage on a success
- Area: 15-foot cone
- Hit: 6d6 cold damage

## Bonus action: Grave Hunger

- Uses: 1/Day
- Target: Self
- Effect: Regain 20 hit points

## Legendary action: Shadow Step

The wight moves up to its speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
