# Flame Dart

- Type: Spell
- Id: flame-dart
- Source: Barrowdale, original homebrew 0.4.0
- Level: Cantrip
- School: Evocation
- Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
- Casting time: Action
- Range: 60 feet
- Components: V, S
- Attack: Ranged spell attack
- Damage: 1d8 fire
- Cantrip scaling: 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 11, 4d8 at level 17

You hurl a dart of flame at a creature or object within range. A flammable object hit by the dart starts burning if it isn't being worn or carried.
