## Spell Details {#barrowdale-flame-dart-details}

Flame Dart is an **Evocation cantrip** for Sorcerers and Wizards.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Casting Time | Action |
| Range | 60 feet |
| Components | V, S |
| Duration | Instantaneous |

You hurl a dart of flame at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes **1d8 Fire damage**. A flammable object hit by the dart starts burning if it isn't being worn or carried.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 11 and 4d8 at level 17.
