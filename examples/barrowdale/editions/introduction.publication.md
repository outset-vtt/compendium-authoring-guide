## The Dale {#barrowdale-introduction-dale}

Barrowdale is a village of sheep farmers and iron diggers in the uplands, built below the great barrow of a forgotten chieftain. [Goblin raiders](compendium:barrowdale:monster:goblin-raider) come down from the hills at dusk, the hill folk keep hearth gifts passed from grandparent to grandchild, and the dale's guides lead travelers through the passes with an [Amulet of Warding](compendium:barrowdale:item:amulet-of-warding) at their throats. Nobody goes into the barrow.

These homebrew entries extend an SRD 5.2.1 base. They are original examples and do not define a new game system. Each entry's rolls, saves, charges and uses execute from its template fields. The table still decides hits, applies damage and adjudicates the text that stays prose.
