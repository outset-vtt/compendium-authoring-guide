## Hill Raider {#barrowdale-goblin-overview}

A Goblin Raider is a Small Fey, Chaotic Neutral, that slips down from the barrow hills at dusk to steal sheep, tools and anything left unguarded.

| Statistic | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Armor Class | 15 |
| Hit Points | 10 (3d6) |
| Speed | 30 feet |
| Challenge | 1/4 (50 XP) |
| Proficiency Bonus | +2 |
| Strength | 8 |
| Dexterity | 14 |
| Constitution | 10 |
| Intelligence | 10 |
| Wisdom | 8 |
| Charisma | 8 |

**Skills** Stealth +6. **Senses** Darkvision 60 feet, Passive Perception 9. **Languages** Common, Goblin.

### Nimble Escape

The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action on each of its turns.

### Scimitar

Melee Attack Roll **+4**, reach **5 feet**, one target; Hit: **5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage**.

### Shortbow

Ranged Attack Roll **+4**, range **80/320 feet**, one target; Hit: **5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage**.

### Loot

A raider sometimes carries a stolen [Healing Draught](compendium:barrowdale:item:healing-draught).
