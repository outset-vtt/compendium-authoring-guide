## Wearing the Amulet {#barrowdale-amulet-use}

An Amulet of Warding is an uncommon wondrous item that requires attunement. It is a silver disc stamped with an old warding rune, worn by the dale's shepherds against things that walk at night.

While you wear the amulet and are attuned to it, you gain a **+1 bonus to Armor Class**.

The amulet has **3 charges**. While wearing and attuned to it, you can take an action and expend **1 charge** to gain **5 temporary hit points**. The amulet regains all expended charges at **dawn**.
