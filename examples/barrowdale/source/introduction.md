# Barrowdale

Barrowdale is a village of sheep farmers and iron diggers in the uplands, built below the great barrow of a forgotten chieftain. Goblins raid down from the hills at dusk, the hill folk keep hearth gifts passed from grandparent to grandchild, and the dale's guides lead travelers through the passes with warding amulets at their throats. Nobody goes into the barrow.

These homebrew entries add a Goblin Raider, a Barrow Wight, the Hill Folk species, the Dale Guide background, an Amulet of Warding, a Boar Spear, Barrow Mail, a Healing Draught, the Keen Watcher feat and the spells Flame Dart, Winter's Breath and Hearth Blessing to an SRD 5.2.1 base. They are original examples and do not define a new game system.

Each entry's rolls, saves, charges and uses execute from its template fields. The table still decides hits, applies damage and adjudicates the text that stays prose: the goblin's Nimble Escape, the wight's Multiattack and Sunlight Sensitivity, and the spells' descriptions.
