## Using the Lantern {#lantern-marsh-lantern-use}

A Signal Lantern is a wondrous item with **3 charges**.

While holding it, you can take an action and expend **1 charge** to send a [Marshlight](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:spell:marshlight) signal for **1 minute**.

The lantern regains all expended charges at **dawn**.

A shutter changes the signal color without expending a charge. A broken lantern cannot send signals.

## Signal Colors {#lantern-marsh-lantern-colors}

| Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Amber | Requests a meeting |
| Blue | Marks a safe route |
