## Using the Lantern {#lantern-marsh-lantern-use}

A Signal Lantern holds **3 glowstones**.

Spend **1 glowstone** to send a [Marshlight](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:spell:marshlight) signal for **2 minutes**.

After a night at a **dry camp**, refill the lantern to **3 glowstones**.

A shutter changes the signal color without spending a glowstone. A broken lantern cannot send signals.

## Signal Colors {#lantern-marsh-lantern-colors}

| Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Amber | Requests a meeting |
| Blue | Marks a safe route |
