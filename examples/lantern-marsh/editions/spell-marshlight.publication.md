## Spell Details {#lantern-marsh-marshlight-details}

Marshlight is a **level 1** spell.

Choose **one visible post within 30 feet**. It shines blue for **2 minutes**, marking a safe route. The light ends early if the post is submerged. It deals **no damage**.
