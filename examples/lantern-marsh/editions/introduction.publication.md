## Field Notes {#lantern-marsh-introduction-notes}

These homebrew field notes add a [Reed Warden](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:monster:reed-warden), a [Signal Lantern](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:item:signal-lantern), and [Marshlight](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:spell:marshlight) to an SRD 5.2.1 base. Travelers mark safe paths with light. Read each entry before using it: the warden's Long Rest limit and the lantern's dawn recharge are separate rules.

The entries are original examples. The Reed Warden includes structured stats and executable Staff attack and damage rolls. Hit adjudication, damage application, critical-hit adjustments, special signals, lantern charges and recovery, and spell effects remain manual. These entries do not define a new game system.
