## Marsh Guardian {#lantern-marsh-warden-overview}

A Reed Warden guards raised paths through the marsh. It has **4 Resolve**.

### Safe-path signal

Once per watch, it can light a safe path for **2 minutes**. It cannot use this signal while submerged.

### Equipment

It carries one [Signal Lantern](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:item:signal-lantern).
