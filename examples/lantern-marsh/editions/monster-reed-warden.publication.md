## Marsh Guardian {#lantern-marsh-warden-overview}

A Reed Warden is a Medium Humanoid that guards raised paths through the marsh.

| Statistic | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Armor Class | 12 |
| Hit Points | 9 |
| Speed | 30 feet |
| Strength | 10 |
| Dexterity | 14 |
| Constitution | 10 |
| Intelligence | 10 |
| Wisdom | 12 |
| Charisma | 10 |

### Staff

Melee Attack Roll **+2**, reach **5 feet**, one target; Hit: **3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage**.

### Safe-path signal

Once per Long Rest, the warden can use an action to light a safe path for **2 minutes**. It cannot use this signal while submerged.

### Equipment

It carries one [Signal Lantern](compendium:lantern-marsh-core:item:signal-lantern).
