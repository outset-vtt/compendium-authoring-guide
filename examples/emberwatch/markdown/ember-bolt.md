# Ember Bolt

- Type: Spell
- Id: ember-bolt
- Source: Emberwatch, original homebrew 0.3.0
- Level: Cantrip
- School: Evocation
- Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
- Casting time: Action
- Range: 90 feet
- Components: V, S
- Attack: Ranged spell attack
- Damage: 1d8 fire
- Cantrip scaling: 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 11, 4d8 at level 17

You flick a mote of watchfire at a creature within range. A flammable object hit by the bolt starts burning if it isn't being worn or carried.
