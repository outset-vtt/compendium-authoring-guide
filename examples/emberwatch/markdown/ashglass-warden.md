- Skills: Perception +4

The tower's last warden, fused into its own ashglass plating.

## Trait: Immutable Form

The warden is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

## Action: Multiattack

The warden makes two Ashglass Fist attacks.

## Action: Ashglass Fist

- Attack: Melee, +8 to hit, reach 10 feet, one target
- Hit: 2d8 + 5 bludgeoning damage
- On hit: Grappled (escape DC 16)

## Action: Cinder Breath

- Uses: Recharge 5-6
- Save: Dexterity 15, half damage on a success
- Area: 30-foot line, 5 feet wide
- Hit: 8d6 fire damage

## Bonus action: Rekindle

- Uses: 1/Day
- Target: Self
- Effect: Regain 20 hit points

## Legendary action: Ember Step

The warden moves up to its speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
