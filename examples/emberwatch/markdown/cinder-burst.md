# Cinder Burst

- Type: Spell
- Id: cinder-burst
- Source: Emberwatch, original homebrew 0.3.0
- Level: 2
- School: Evocation
- Classes: Sorcerer, Wizard
- Casting time: Action
- Range: Self
- Components: V, S, M (a pinch of watchfire ash)
- Save: Dexterity, half damage on a success
- Area: 15-foot cone
- Damage: 3d8 fire
- Higher levels: +1d8 damage per slot level above 2

A fan of embers erupts from your hands. Unattended flammable objects in the area start burning.
