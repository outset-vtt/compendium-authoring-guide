A brass skeleton wrapped in ashglass plates patrols the abandoned signal towers of Emberwatch.

## Trait: Ashglass Plating

When the sentinel takes fire damage, its plates glow for one minute and shed enough light to read by. The table decides whether nearby tinder catches.

## Action: Ash Spear

- Attack: Melee, +5 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target
- Hit: 1d8 + 3 piercing damage

## Bonus action: Cinder Guard

- Target: Self
- Effect: Gain 5 temporary hit points
