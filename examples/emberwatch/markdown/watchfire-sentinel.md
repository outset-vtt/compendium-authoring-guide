# Watchfire Sentinel

- Type: Feat
- Id: watchfire-sentinel
- Source: Emberwatch, original homebrew 0.3.0
- Category: General
- Prerequisite: Level 4+
- Ability score increase: +1 Wisdom or Constitution

You have kept the long watch on the Emberwatch towers, and little escapes your notice.

## Passive: Long Watch

- Effect: +2 to ranged weapon attack rolls
- Effect: Advantage on Perception checks

## Bonus action: Signal Fire

- Target: Self
- Effect: Gain 5 temporary hit points

## Trait: Tower Sense

You always know which direction the nearest signal tower lies, if one is within a mile.
