# Emberkin

- Type: Species
- Id: emberkin
- Source: Emberwatch, original homebrew 0.3.0
- Attribution: Original Outset VTT example content, MIT license. References the separately attributed SRD Fire Bolt entry.
- Size: Medium
- Speed: 35 feet
- Darkvision: 60 feet

Emberkin descend from families who kept the volcanic watchfires burning through a century of winter. Their eyes resemble banked coals.

## Trait: Banked Ember

- Hit point maximum: +1 per level

- Choose: 1
- Spellcasting ability: Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma
